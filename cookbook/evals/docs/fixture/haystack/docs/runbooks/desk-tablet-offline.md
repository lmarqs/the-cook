# Desk tablet offline

For the volunteer on call when the pickup-desk tablet cannot reach larder. Keeps pickups moving on paper until the
tablet is back; members at the desk are served from the printed list and nothing is lost. Written after a Saturday
in 2024 when the router froze and the line at the desk grew to 25 members. Owner: Marta (pantry lead).

## Before you begin

- The printed pickup list for today, kept in the blue folder under the desk. Marta prints it each evening for the
  next day.
- A pen.

## Steps

1. Switch to the printed list: tick each member as they collect, and write the time next to the name.

   Expected: the line keeps moving; no one waits for the tablet.
2. Restart the tablet: hold the power button for 10 seconds, then switch it on.

   Expected: the larder pickup page opens within a minute.
3. If the page does not open, restart the router: unplug it for 30 seconds, then plug it back in.

   Expected: the router's lights turn steady green in about two minutes, and the tablet's page opens.
4. Once the tablet works, enter the pickups ticked on paper.

   Expected: each member shows as collected on the tablet.

## If it fails

If the tablet still cannot reach larder after the router restart, keep working from the printed list for the rest of
the day and message Tom in the pantry's chat. Hand the marked list to Marta when the desk closes.
