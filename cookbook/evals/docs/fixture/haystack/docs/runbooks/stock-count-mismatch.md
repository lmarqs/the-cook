# Stock count mismatch

For the volunteer on call when the shelf count and larder's stock disagree after an intake. Brings larder's count for
an item back to what is on the shelf, so members see the right items when they pick their pantry orders. Written
after the autumn 2024 intake, when larder showed 40 tins of chickpeas that the shelf did not have. Owner: Marta
(pantry lead).

## Before you begin

- The intake sheet signed by the volunteers who unloaded the delivery.
- Access to the stock page in larder's admin area.

## Steps

1. Recount the item on the shelf, twice, ideally with a second volunteer.

   Expected: two equal counts.
2. Open the item in the stock page and compare its count with the shelf count.

   Expected: the page shows larder's count and the date of the last change.
3. Enter the shelf count in the stock page, with the reason, such as `intake miscount`.

   Expected: the page shows the new count and records your name and the reason.
4. Add a line to the monthly report sheet: the item, the two counts and the reason.

   Expected: the line is on the sheet for Marta's report.

## If it fails

If the recount differs from the intake sheet by more than 10 percent of the delivery, leave the stock as it is and
ask Marta; the delivery may be missing items. If the stock page will not save, ask Tom.
