# Duplicate charge

For the volunteer on call when a member reports being charged twice for one pantry order. Returns the money charged
the second time and tells the member; the first charge stays, and the pantry order is unaffected. Written after the
2024 reports, when the volunteers answered each member differently. Owner: the payments volunteers.

## Before you begin

- Access to the card gateway's dashboard.
- The member's name and the pantry order they mean.

## Steps

1. In the card gateway's dashboard, search the member's name and find the two captures.

   Expected: two charges of the same amount for the same pantry order, with different times.
2. Refund the later charge in the dashboard, following [Refunds](../features/refunds.md).

   Expected: the charge shows as refunded.
3. Tell the member the later charge was refunded, and that their bank may take up to five working days to show it.

   Expected: the member replies, or the report is closed in the pantry's chat.

## If it fails

If you find only one charge, or two charges of different amounts, do not refund; ask the payments volunteers in the
pantry's chat. Nadia handles anything a member disputes with their bank.
