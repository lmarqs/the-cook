# Volunteer accounts

For whoever creates or removes a volunteer's login, or changes what a role may do. A volunteer account is created by
an admin, carries one or more of the roles desk, driver, payments and admin, and is removed entirely, with the
volunteer's rota history, when the volunteer leaves. Written when a former driver's account was found still active
eight months after she left.

## Creating an account

Only a volunteer with the admin role creates accounts: today Ana, Tom and Marta. The admin enters the volunteer's name
and email address and ticks the roles; larder emails a link that lets the volunteer choose a password and expires after
48 hours. A volunteer is not a member, and a volunteer who also orders from the pantry has a separate member
registration. The PIN of the pickup-desk tablet is separate from this login; see [volunteer login](volunteer-login.md).

## Roles

- **Desk**: sees today's pantry orders and marks them picked up.
- **Driver**: sees the delivery list for the day and marks deliveries done.
- **Payments**: reads captured pantry orders and the card gateway's charge ids; Nadia holds this role with Tom.
- **Admin**: creates accounts, opens and closes pickup slots, and reads the feedback.

A volunteer's roles are shown on their account page; changing them is an admin's action and is written to the
admin log with the name of the admin who made it.

## When a volunteer leaves

The admin removes the account on the volunteer's last day, in one action. The account is deleted entirely: the login,
the roles and the volunteer's rota history, because the pantry has no use for a former volunteer's record. Entries in
the admin log that mention the volunteer are kept with the name replaced by "former volunteer".
