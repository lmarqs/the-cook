# Stock intake

For whoever receives the food bank's Monday delivery, or changes how stock is counted in larder. A volunteer counts each
delivered item, notes its expiry date and enters the count in larder, which raises the stock. Written after a pallet of
yoghurt reached its expiry date on the shelf because nobody had recorded it.

## Receiving

The food bank's van arrives on Monday from 9:00. Two volunteers, usually Leo and Priya, check each crate against the
delivery note, count the items and sort them by expiry date. A crate with damaged items is set aside and noted for
Marta, who tells the food bank the same day.

## Entering the stock

In the intake page a volunteer picks the item, enters the count and the earliest expiry date of that count, and saves.
Larder adds the count to the item's stock and keeps the date; the intake page lists items that expire within 14 days in
amber, so the desk puts them on the front shelf. An item that arrives for the first time is created in the catalog
first, as [Pantry items](pantry-items.md) describes. Tom reviews the intake page every Monday at 14:00, and any item
still missing a count by then goes to the next week's list.

## Shaped by

- [Pantry items](pantry-items.md), the catalog whose stock the intake raises.
- [Allergens](allergens.md), which a new item must carry before it is listed.
