# Price list

For whoever changes how item prices are set in the catalog, or answers a volunteer whose price change did not appear.
A volunteer updates an item's price in the price list page, prices are rounded to 10 cents, a second volunteer approves
each change, and every change is logged. Written after a volunteer typed 4.50 for 0.45 and the item sat at ten times its
price for a morning.

## Changing a price

A volunteer opens the item in the price list, types the new price and submits it. Larder rounds the price to the nearest
10 cents, so 0.43 becomes 0.40, and shows the rounded value before the change is sent for approval. The item keeps its
current price until the change is approved.

## Approval and log

A second volunteer, never the one who typed the price, sees the pending changes in the price list page and approves or
rejects each one with a reason. Tom and Marta approve most of them. Every change is logged with the item, the old and
the new price, who typed it, who approved it and when, and the log page lists the last 90 days. A change that lowers or
raises a price by more than 50 percent is marked in red for the approver.

## Shaped by

- [Pantry items](pantry-items.md), the catalog the prices belong to.
