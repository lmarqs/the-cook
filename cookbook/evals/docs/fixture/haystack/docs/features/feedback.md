# Feedback

For whoever reads or changes the feedback form. After a pantry order is picked up, the member can send feedback in one
short form; volunteers read it every week. Written after complaints sent to the pantry's shared inbox were lost under
the donation receipts and a member who had been given spoiled milk heard nothing for a month.

## The form

The form is reached from a link on the pickup confirmation page. It asks for a score from 1 to 5, a free-text comment
of up to 1,000 characters, and a tick box, "Please contact me". The form is linked to the pantry order, so a volunteer
can see which pickup it was about, but the comment is stored apart from the member's name in the list volunteers read.

## Weekly reading

Every Monday morning Marta reads the week's feedback in the admin dashboard. Entries with a score of 2 or less, or the
tick box set, are shown first. Marta assigns each of those to a volunteer, usually Leo for the desk and Priya for
drivers, and the volunteer replies to the member within three days and marks the entry done. Entries nobody has marked
done by the next Monday are shown again at the top.

## What it replaced

Feedback no longer goes to the shared inbox: the pantry's address now answers with a line pointing to the form, so a
complaint has one place to land. Ana adds the average score of the month to the figures Marta brings to the volunteers'
monthly meeting.
