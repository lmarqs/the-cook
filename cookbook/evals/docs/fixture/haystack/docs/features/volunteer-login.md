# Volunteer login

For whoever changes how volunteers log in to the pickup-desk tablet, or answers a volunteer locked out of it. A
volunteer logs in with a four-digit PIN, and the tablet login lasts one shift: 8 hours at most, ending sooner when the
shift closes. Written after the Saturday tablet stayed logged in overnight and the Sunday cleaners could open the pickup
list.

## PIN

Each volunteer chooses a four-digit PIN at the first shift, and Tom resets it from the rota page when it is forgotten.
Five wrong PINs in a row lock that volunteer's login for 10 minutes. The PIN opens only the pickup-desk screens: marking
a pantry order as collected and looking up a pickup slot. It does not open the price list or the card gateway's
dashboard.

## One shift

The tablet login ends 8 hours after it began, or when the shift lead taps "Close shift" on the tablet, whichever comes
first. The overnight case came from a shift that nobody closed and a limit that was then 24 hours. The tablet shows the
time left in its corner, and a volunteer who works a double shift logs in again at the break.
