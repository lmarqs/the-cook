# Pickup slots

For whoever changes when members collect their pantry orders, or answers a member who cannot find a slot. Pickup slots
start every 15 minutes on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, and each holds at most 12 pantry orders. The slot page shows
what is free. Written after the Saturday queue reached the street when 40 members arrived for the same quarter hour.

## Slots

Each pickup day has slots from 10:00 to 16:00, 24 slots a day, so 288 pantry orders at most. A member picks a slot at
checkout from the slot page, which lists the free places in each slot and greys out the full ones. Tomás or Leo may
raise one slot's limit for a day when the desk has extra volunteers; the page then shows the new number.

## The Saturday queue

Before the change members chose a day and arrived at any time, and the Saturday morning crowd formed outside the hall.
With slots of 15 minutes and a limit of 12, the desk serves a slot in about 12 minutes and the queue stays inside.
Larder asks members to come no earlier than five minutes before their slot, and the desk marks a pantry order as
collected when it is handed over.
