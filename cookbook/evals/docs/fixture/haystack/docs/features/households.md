# Households

For whoever changes how larder groups members, or answers a member placed in a household they do not belong to. Members
at one address form a household. Larder matches addresses on the normalised street, the number and the flat, and a
volunteer can split a household that was matched by mistake. Written after two neighbours in one building were merged.

## Matching

When a member registers, larder normalises the address: lower case, no punctuation, "street" for "st." and "road" for
"rd", and the number and flat are kept apart. Two members whose normalised street, number and flat are equal join one
household. A different flat in the same building is a different household. Larder never matches on surname, because
relatives often live apart and strangers often share a name.

## Splitting a household

Marta or Priya opens the household in the volunteer pages and chooses "Split". Larder asks which members leave, creates
a new household for them and keeps every pantry order with the member who made it. A split can be undone by merging
the two households again. The merged neighbours of the first case were split in this way in 2024 and have stayed apart.
