# Volunteer rota

For whoever changes the volunteer shift rota, or answers a volunteer who missed a shift. Each Sunday at 18:00 larder
emails every volunteer a digest of their shifts for the coming week, which volunteers asked for in 2024. Swaps happen in
the rota page. Written after two volunteers each assumed the other had the Thursday desk and nobody opened it.

## The rota

Priya keeps the rota in larder: one row per shift, with a day, a start and end time, a role such as desk, stock or
driver, and the volunteers who hold it. Pickup days have three shifts and the Monday intake has one. A shift with fewer
volunteers than its role needs shows in red on the rota page, and Marta reads that list every Friday.

## The Sunday digest

The digest lists a volunteer's shifts for Monday to Sunday and links to the rota page. It lists only shifts, nothing
about pantry orders or members. A volunteer who wants no digest turns it off in their profile; around 15 of the 60
active volunteers have. The worker sends it at 18:00 on Sunday from the queue in Postgres.

## Swaps

A volunteer who cannot work a shift offers it in the rota page, and any volunteer with the same role can take it. The
rota keeps the swap for the next digest, and Marta is told when a shift is offered for less than 24 hours.
