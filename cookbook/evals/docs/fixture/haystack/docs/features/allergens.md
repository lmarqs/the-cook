# Allergens

For whoever changes how items are tagged with allergens, or answers a member who received an item they cannot eat. Items
are tagged with the 14 EU allergens, and a member's allergy filter hides tagged items from the catalog. Written after a
member with a peanut allergy received a mixed bag that held a peanut-butter jar.

## Tags

Each item carries any of the 14 EU allergens, such as peanuts, milk, gluten or sesame. A volunteer tags a new item in
the catalog before it is listed, and larder refuses to list an item whose tags were never set, not even to "none". The
peanut-butter jar had been entered as a spread with no tags at all, and the check for that case came afterwards.

## The allergy filter

In their profile a member ticks the allergens they avoid. The catalog then hides every item with one of those tags, and
larder blocks a pantry order that holds a tagged item of the member's allergens. Mixed bags are packed by the desk, so
Marta packs them from the filtered list and not from the shelf. Tags are a volunteer's reading of the label, so a member
with a severe allergy is still told to check the packaging.
