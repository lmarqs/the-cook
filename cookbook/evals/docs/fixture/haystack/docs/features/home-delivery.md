# Home delivery

For whoever changes deliveries for housebound members, or answers a member whose delivery went wrong. Volunteer drivers
bring a member's pantry order to their door on Wednesdays, and the member can change the delivery's address and time
window until 18:00 the day before. Written after a driver went to an old address that the member had already replaced.

## Who is delivered

A member is marked housebound by Marta after a call or a note from a carer. A housebound member picks "Home delivery" at
checkout instead of a pickup slot, and gives an address and one of three time windows: 9:00 to 11:00, 11:00 to 13:00 or
13:00 to 15:00. Each driver takes up to 8 stops in a morning, and members of one household at the same address share one
stop.

## Changing a delivery

Until 18:00 on Tuesday the member can edit the address and the time window from their profile. At 18:00 larder freezes
the list and prints one route sheet per driver; a change after that needs a call to Marta, who edits the sheet by hand.
The driver's sheet is the only place that holds the frozen address, so the old-address trip came from a sheet printed
before the member's edit was saved. Larder now prints the sheets after the last edit is stored.

## Shaped by

- [Households](households.md), which decides whether two members at one address share a driver.
