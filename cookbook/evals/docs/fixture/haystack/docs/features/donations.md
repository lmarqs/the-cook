# Donations

For whoever changes the donate page or answers a donor charged unexpectedly. The public site takes card donations, one
off or monthly, through the same card gateway that charges pantry orders. Donors are not members: a donation needs an
email address for the thank-you note and nothing else. Written when a donor wrote to ask whether a monthly donation had
stopped, and nobody could tell from the pages which kind it was.

## Giving once or monthly

The donate page offers 10, 25 and 50 euros or a free amount from 5 to 500 euros, and a switch between one-off and
monthly. A one-off donation is charged when the donor confirms. A monthly donation is charged the same day every month
until the donor stops it, from the link at the bottom of each thank-you email; the link needs no login because a
donor has no account.

## What larder keeps

Larder stores the donor's email, the amount, the day and the gateway's charge id, and no card details: the card number
goes from the donor's browser to the card gateway and never reaches larder. Donations live in their own table and are
never mixed into the counts for pantry orders or members. Nadia reads the table at the end of each month to match it
against the food bank's bank statement, and Tom exports a yearly total for the food bank's annual report.

## Where it differs from pantry orders

A donation has no pantry order, no pickup slot and no capture step: the gateway charges the card in one call. The
thank-you email is sent by the worker, as other background emails are. A donor who writes to the volunteers is asked
for the email address used, which is the only way to find the donation.
