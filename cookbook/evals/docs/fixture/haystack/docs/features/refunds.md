# Refunds

For whoever changes how a member is refunded, or answers a member whose item was damaged or missing. A payments
volunteer issues the refund from the card gateway's dashboard, never from larder, and partial refunds are allowed. Every
refund carries its own key, the refund's id. Written after a member received a crushed jar of sauce and nobody could say
whether the refund had already gone out.

## Issuing a refund

Nadia or another payments volunteer opens the pantry order in the gateway's dashboard, picks the damaged or missing
items and enters the amount, which cannot exceed what the pantry order was charged. The dashboard shows the refund's id
at once, and the volunteer writes it in the pantry order's note in larder. A member who reports a problem within a week
of pickup is refunded; a later report goes to Nadia, who decides.

## One key per refund

The refund's id is its key: a second click on the same refund returns the first one, and a second refund for the same
pantry order, such as for another missing item, has its own id and its own key. Several partial refunds may add up to
the charged amount and no more. The sauce case went wrong because the note was empty, so larder now shows the refund ids
in the pantry order's page.
