# Cancellation

For whoever changes when a member may cancel a pantry order, or answers a member who could not. A member cancels a
pantry order from the app until a cutoff before pickup. Written after an autumn of packed bags left on the shelf.

## Cutoff

<!-- fact:cutoff -->
A member can cancel a pantry order until 2 hours before its pickup slot starts, and not after: the volunteers pack the
bags in those 2 hours, and in October 2025 cancellations made after packing left 25 bags of fresh food to spoil.
<!-- /fact:cutoff -->
