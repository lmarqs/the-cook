# Ordering limits

For whoever changes who may order from the pantry, or answers a member told they cannot order. Larder limits the
open pantry orders a household holds, so that one household cannot empty the shelves. Written after a week in 2025
when the shelves were empty by Wednesday.

## Open orders

A pantry order is open from confirmation until it is picked up or cancelled.

## Households

<!-- fact:household-limit -->
A household, the members registered at the same address, holds at most 2 open pantry orders at a time, counted
across all its members: in March 2025 one family ordered under four members' names, and the pantry was empty by
Wednesday for the other 60 households.
