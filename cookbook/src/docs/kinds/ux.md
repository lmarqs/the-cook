## Standards, `ux/`

Holds the rules every interface follows, such as naming, messages and output. Diátaxis type: reference, normative.

- The line after the title states the scope: which interfaces the standard covers.
- Rules use MUST, SHOULD and MAY in capitals, and sparingly.
- Each rule carries its reason in one sentence, or links the record in `adr/` that holds it.
- A standard is edited in place as its rules change.

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT
RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" in the standards under `ux/` are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14
(RFC 2119, RFC 8174) when, and only when, they appear in all capitals, as shown here.
