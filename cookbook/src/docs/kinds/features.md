## Features, `features/`

Holds what the system does, one page per unit of behavior. Diátaxis type: reference.

What makes a good feature page:

- Opens with a lead that says what the unit is and why it exists, or why it is as it is: what went wrong, or could not
  be done, without it.
- Follows the unit's own structure, its inputs, states and outputs as it has them, so a reader finds a fact where the
  product puts it. Examples help.
- Describes. The options weighed for a foundation decision and the steps to operate the unit are facts of other kinds.
- Lists, in `Known limits`, what the unit doesn't handle, handles only partly or handles in a way that catches a reader
  out, each with its workaround. A limit leaves the page in the change that lifts it.
