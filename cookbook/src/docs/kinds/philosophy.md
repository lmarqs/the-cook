## Philosophy, `philosophy.md`

Holds the tenets that guide the decisions: what the team favors when options conflict. Diátaxis type: explanation.

- One page.
- A `Tenets` section: each tenet in one sentence, what it rules out, and links to the records in `adrs/` that apply it.
  A tenet that rules nothing out cannot guide a decision.
- A tenet guides many decisions; one decision applying it is a record in `adrs/`.
