## Philosophy, `philosophy.md`

Holds the tenets that guide the decisions: what the team favors when two good options conflict. Diátaxis type:
explanation.

What makes a good philosophy page:

- Is one page, with a `Tenets` section.
- States each tenet in one sentence, then what it rules out. A tenet that rules nothing out, such as "quality
  matters", cannot guide a decision.
- Shows each tenet at work in the decisions that apply it, so a reader can judge whether it still holds. A tenet
  guides many decisions; one decision applying it is a decision record.
